Summary

SoftShell is an SSH / Mosh client. NoffTech does not run a SoftShell account system and does not operate a SoftShell backend that receives your session data. Connection details, passwords, and keys stay on your device (and, if you turn on Full Version iCloud Keychain sync, in your iCloud Keychain). Network traffic goes to the hosts you choose, not to NoffTech. Purchases go through Apple. We do not show ads and we do not use tracking SDKs.

What we collect

NoffTech does not collect personal information from SoftShell for our own servers. SoftShell’s privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types for the developer.

Third parties

  • Apple — App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, (optional) iCloud Keychain, system Face ID / Keychain / Local Network APIs. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s processing.
  • Hosts you connect to — You choose them. Their privacy and security practices are outside NoffTech’s control.
  • Open-source libraries (Citadel, SwiftTerm, swift-mosh, SwiftNIO SSH stack, and related packages) run on your device as part of SoftShell. They are not used to phone home to NoffTech.

Retention and deletion

Because SoftShell data is on your device (and optionally your iCloud Keychain), you control deletion:

  • Delete hosts, keys, and snippets in the app.
  • Turn off Face ID lock in Settings.
  • Delete SoftShell from the device to remove the app container.
  • For Keychain items, remove SoftShell data / Keychain entries as exposed by the app and iOS Settings, and manage iCloud Keychain from your Apple Account devices if you enabled sync.
  • Manage or refund App Store purchases through Apple.

If you contact us about privacy, we may retain your email and the content of the request as needed to respond.

Children’s privacy

SoftShell is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

California / similar laws

If SoftShell does not collect personal information about you onto NoffTech systems, many “sale,” “share,” and access/deletion request concepts have limited application. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are commonly used under the CCPA/CPRA. To ask a privacy question, use the contact below.

International users

SoftShell is offered from the United States. If you use it abroad, you are responsible for complying with local laws that apply to your use of remote-access software and to the hosts you connect to.
